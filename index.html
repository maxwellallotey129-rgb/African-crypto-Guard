Common African Crypto & P2P Scams

Recognize these warning signs in crypto trades and online investment pitches. Payment processes and consumer protections vary by country and provider.

  1. 01

    Mobile money (MoMo) reversal scams

    A caller or message claims a payment was sent to you by mistake, shows a fake reversal alert, or pressures you to send money back to another number. Check: contact your provider through its official app or number, and do not refund based on a caller, SMS, or screenshot.

  2. 02

    WhatsApp / Telegram investment groups

    A group promises guaranteed crypto profits, shares fabricated success screenshots, or pressures members to pay an upfront fee or recruit others. Check: treat guaranteed returns as a red flag, verify firms independently through official sources, and never share your PIN, seed phrase, or one-time code.

  3. 03

    Fake P2P bank transfer receipts

    A buyer sends an edited screenshot, email, or SMS as proof of a bank transfer that has not arrived or cleared. Check: confirm the funds in your own bank account and follow the platform’s release process; never rely on a receipt image alone.

  4. 04

    Third-party or mismatched payments

    The payer asks you to accept money from a different person or number, making the trade harder to verify. Check: follow the platform’s identity and payment-name rules; don’t move a trade off-platform.

  5. 05

    Fake escrow, agents, or support

    An impersonator offers to hold funds, resolve a trade, or speed up a withdrawal through a private chat or link. Check: use only the platform’s official escrow and support channels.

  6. 06

    PIN, OTP, or approval-code requests

    Someone posing as a buyer, provider, or support agent asks for a secret code or tells you to approve a prompt. Check: never share your mobile-money PIN or one-time code, and don’t approve a request you didn’t initiate.

  7. 07

    SIM-swap or account takeover

    Your phone suddenly loses service, or you receive unexpected account-reset messages while someone tries to access your mobile-money account. Check: contact your mobile operator and provider using their official channels immediately.

If a trade feels rushed or a payment status is unclear, pause and contact your provider through its official app or published number.
